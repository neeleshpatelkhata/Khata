Privacy Policy — Khata Ledger Enterprise

Last Updated: September 14, 2026

Khata Ledger Enterprise ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your financial and personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, stores, and safeguards your information when you use the Khata Ledger mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Full name, email address, phone number, workspace name, and user role (Owner or Staff).
  • Financial & Ledger Data: Transaction amounts, payment modes (Cash, UPI, Bank Transfer, Cheque), categories, itemized GST tax data, credit/debit balances, and notes.
  • Party / Contact Records: Customer and supplier contact names, phone numbers, email addresses, and location addresses entered into your digital ledger.
  • Receipt Images & OCR Scans: Images of bills or invoices uploaded via camera or file selection for automated AI optical character recognition (OCR) processing.
  • Device Telemetry & Crash Logs: Technical error diagnostic details and app performance metrics to identify runtime issues post-launch.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To compute accurate running balances, net receivable ("You Will Get"), and net payable ("You Will Give") totals.
  • To provide automated AI bill extraction via optical character recognition.
  • To enable multi-party contact management and account statement timelines.
  • To enforce Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) preventing unauthorized deletion or alteration of entries.
  • To sync data across offline local storage and encrypted cloud backup databases.

3. Data Security & Storage

Your data is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage structures and synchronized over SSL/TLS encrypted channels to high-security PostgreSQL cloud infrastructure (Supabase). We do not sell or share your financial data with third-party advertisers.

4. App Permissions Required

  • Camera: Required only when taking photos of bills or invoices for the AI Photo Scanner feature.
  • Storage / Media: Required to attach receipt documents or save exported backup statement files.
  • Internet Access: Required to sync cloud backups and authenticate user sessions.

5. Data Retention & Soft Delete

Records marked for archiving or deletion are stored as soft-deleted items to ensure financial historical reporting accuracy and compliance. Users may export or clear their data at any time via the Backup Telemetry Center.
